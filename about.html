Personal Ties to the United States

From being a resident and student living within the United States, my connection to the country is based on experience of interacting with its varied physical geography. Being a resident of the Intermountain West region, the area where I live boasts some amazing physical geography from desert basins at high altitude to mountain ranges of alpine heights.

Physical Geography Highlight: The Great Basin

The most outstanding physical geography feature of the United States is the Great Basin, which is a huge endorheic drainage system that covers the major part of Nevada, as well as parts of Utah, California, Oregon, and Idaho. Being internally draining basin, no water that falls within the Great Basin basin flows out to the ocean.

Rather, it gets evaporated, seeps underground or gathers in the form of terminal lakes such as the Great Salt Lake.

Spiritual Reflection: Alma 30:44

In seeing how physical systems work together to maintain biological existence, one can understand that there is an order and design behind such systems and not mere chaos and randomness. The magnificence of the earth’s physical systems becomes a witness of God’s creation.
